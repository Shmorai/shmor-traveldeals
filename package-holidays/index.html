Package holidays

Flights, hotel and often transfers in one booking. Compare package holiday deals and see when a package beats booking it all yourself.

What is a package holiday?

A package holiday combines at least two travel services, usually flights and accommodation, sold together. Many packages also include airport transfers and a board basis such as half board or all-inclusive.

How packages can protect you

Packages often come with extra protection. In the UK, flight-inclusive packages from UK businesses are usually protected under the ATOL scheme, and the Package Travel Regulations make the organiser responsible for the whole package. In the EU, the Package Travel Directive gives similar rights. In the US, there’s no single equivalent, so check the provider’s terms and consider travel insurance.

Always confirm the protection on the provider’s site before you pay.

Package or do it yourself?

Packages are convenient and can be cheaper, especially for late deals and peak family dates. Booking flights and hotels separately gives you more flexibility over times, airlines and where you stay. Compare both: the cheaper option changes from trip to trip.

What to check before booking

  • Flight times, airline and luggage allowance
  • Transfer length and type (shared or private)
  • Board basis and what it covers
  • Payment schedule, deposit and cancellation terms
  • Financial protection details

Frequently asked questions

Are package holidays cheaper than booking separately?
Sometimes. Operators can bundle discounted flights and rooms, but for some trips booking separately is cheaper. Compare both for your dates.
What is ATOL protection?
ATOL is a UK scheme that protects many flight-inclusive holidays sold by UK businesses. If the company fails, it helps you get home or get your money back.
Can I pay for a package holiday with a deposit?
Many providers offer low-deposit options, with the balance due some weeks before departure. Check the payment schedule and cancellation terms first.
