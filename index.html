How to find a genuinely good travel deal

  • Be flexible. Moving your dates by a few days, or flying midweek, can make a real difference to the price.
  • Compare like for like. Check what’s included: luggage, transfers, meals and taxes all change the true cost.
  • Know when demand peaks. School holidays and major events push prices up. Book those dates early, or travel just outside them.
  • Check the cancellation terms. A slightly higher refundable rate can be better value than a non-refundable one if your plans might change.
  • Check how you’re protected. Package holidays often come with extra financial protection. See package holidays.

Travelling from the US, UK or Europe

TravelDeals is built for travellers on both sides of the Atlantic. Whether you call it a holiday or a vacation, the same rules apply: compare the total price, read the terms, and check entry requirements for your destination before you book.

Consumer protections differ by country. In the UK, many flight-inclusive packages are protected under the ATOL scheme. In the EU, the Package Travel Directive covers packages. In the US, protection depends on how you pay and the provider’s own terms, so travel insurance is worth considering.

Frequently asked questions

Is TravelDeals a travel agent?
No. TravelDeals is a free comparison and discovery site. When you choose a deal, you book directly with the travel company or booking site, under their terms.
Does it cost anything to use TravelDeals?
No. TravelDeals is free and never adds a booking fee. Some links are affiliate links, so the provider may pay us a commission when you book. This doesn’t change your price.
Are the prices I see final?
Prices and availability are set by providers and can change quickly. Always check the final price, including taxes, fees and luggage, on the provider’s site before you pay.
Is my holiday protected if I book through a link here?
Your protection depends on what you book and who you book with, not on how you found it. Packages often have extra protection (such as ATOL in the UK or the EU Package Travel Directive). For flights or hotels booked separately, check the provider’s terms and consider travel insurance.
